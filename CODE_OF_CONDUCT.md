# Code of conduct

Poise is built through precise, good-faith technical collaboration. Everyone
participating in its repositories, issue trackers, reviews, discussions, and
community spaces is expected to:

- treat people with respect regardless of background, identity, experience, or
  communication style;
- critique ideas and evidence without attacking the person presenting them;
- make room for questions, corrections, and dissent supported by reasoning;
- avoid harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, doxxing,
  and sustained disruption;
- protect private security reports and personal information;
- accept maintainer direction on scope, tone, and venue.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, limit participation, or ban an
account when necessary to protect the project and its participants. Enforcement
decisions should be proportionate, documented privately, and avoid retaliation.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's GitHub contact
channel. Do not use a public issue when doing so would expose the reporter or
another participant.
